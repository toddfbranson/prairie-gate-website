Meet Todd Branson


Todd is an association marketing and operations leader with 15+ years growing membership organizations across the agriculture and equine sectors.Todd has a track record of driving membership growth (+5% in a single year), building corporate sponsorship revenue, scaling youth development programs backed by $1M+ in secured funding, and directing large-scale championship events. His career skillset includes brand strategy, multi-channel campaigns, nonprofit board relations, and fiscal management. Todd has found gold in all demographic sectors including rebuilding rural county programs (1,000%+ budget growth) to relocating an international headquarters. Todd's overarching goal is to help boards and nonprofits focus on their passions while he creates new innovative strategic marketing pathways, reimagines partnership development, and provides guidance to enhance program leadership to all mission-driven agricultural and rural associations.


"My life goal is to help nonprofits and associations build new collaboration pathways, build new partnerships and supporters, and to watch them celebrate their passions while sharing with others about their organization's mission."
